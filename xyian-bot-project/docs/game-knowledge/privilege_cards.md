# Privilege Cards

A set of cards (some lifetime, some monthly) that provide ongoing daily rewards and quality-of-life perks. Accessed from the **Privilege Card** icon on the main screen left panel.

## Free daily

### Daily Gifts (free, available to everyone)
- **Daily payout:** 10 gems/day
- Resets daily

## Lifetime cards (one-time purchase)

These are the standout long-term value purchases — buy once, benefit forever.

> **Pricing note:** The numbers shown next to "Purchase and get" on the card screens (1,200 / 3,900) are **one-time gem bonuses you receive on purchase**, NOT the prices. Actual prices are not visible to players who already own the cards. Pricing currency (USD or Aurocite) and exact amounts are TBD — needs a non-owner's view to capture.

| Card | One-time bonus on purchase | Daily payout | Other benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lifetime Ad-Free Card** | 1,200 gems | 20 gems/day | Ad-free forever; increases daily free Gems |
| **Lifetime Supply Card** | 3,900 gems | **800 gems/day** | Claim tons of gems daily |

## Monthly cards (30-day subscriptions)

- Each card lasts 30 days.
- **Renewal cost: 499 Aurocite** (NOT gems).
- Initial purchase comes with a 500-gem one-time bonus (see "Purchase and get" on screen). Initial purchase price not visible to current owners.

### Monthly Card
- **Daily payout:** 400 gems
- New skill refreshes in battle +1
- Forfeit skill selection
- Skippable arena battles
- Skip Peak Arena battle

### Hunting Warrior Card
- **Daily payout:** 100 gems
- Unlimited multiple Quick Hunts
- **Max Energy +20**
- AFK Rewards +20%

### Cave Explorer Card
- **Daily payout:** None
- Gold Cave Daily Challenges +2
- Unlock Auto-Dig in Rune Ruins

### Sealing Master Card
- **Daily payout:** None
- Seal Battle Daily Challenges +1
- Seal Battle can be Quick-Raided with the highest DMG in this round
- Monster Invasion can be Quick-Raided with DMG dealt in the 1st round of the current day

### Abyssium Conqueror Card
- **Daily payout:** None
- Abyssal Tide can be Quick-Raided with the highest DMG dealt in the current day
- Daily Abyssal Tide challenges +1
- Cleared Shackled Jungle stages can be Quick Raided
- Daily Shackled Jungle challenges +2
- Daily Sky Tower challenges +3

## Recommendation framing (for the bot)

**Default rule: Do NOT recommend purchases to F2P players.** It gets annoying fast and is the wrong frame for that audience.

**For F2P players:**
- Focus advice on free progression, daily routines, and best use of free gems.
- Lifetime cards may be mentioned only if the player asks specifically about value, or if the situation clearly calls for it (e.g., they say "I'm willing to spend a little, what's worth it?"). Even then, frame as optional.

**For paying / open-to-paying players:**
- **Lifetime Ad-Free + Lifetime Supply** are the two purchases broadly worth recommending to anyone playing actively — they pay back over time.
- **Monthly cards are situational** — match to player activity:
  - Heavy Quick Hunts user → Hunting Warrior (energy cap + AFK boost)
  - Active in Gold Cave / Rune Ruins → Cave Explorer
  - Active in Seal Battle / Monster Invasion → Sealing Master
  - Active in Abyssal Tide / Shackled Jungle / Sky Tower → Abyssium Conqueror
  - Heavy Arena player → Monthly Card (skip features + skill refresh)
