# Hunt

An idle / AFK reward system accessed from the **Hunt** button on the main screen. Replaces the need to grind earlier stages by paying out rewards passively.

## How it works

- **Auto-get rewards over time** — accumulates rewards passively while not playing.
- **Max accumulation: 24 hours.** After 24h the buffer stops filling — claim before then to avoid wasted time.
- **Later chapters grant better rewards** — Hunt rate scales with campaign progression. Push deeper to increase per-hour yield.
- Reward types accumulated: **Gold**, **EXP**, plus a mix of **gear, gear scrolls, and runes** (probabilistic — see drop rates below). Hunt does not drop hero/character shards.

## Two ways to collect

### 1. Claim (the green button)
Collects whatever has accumulated up to 24h.

### 2. Quick Hunt (the blue button)
**Instantly** receive Hunting rewards without waiting. Two options:

| Option | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Free (watch ad)** | 1 ad per use | Base **2 free chances per day**; can earn more from various sources. **Hunting Warrior Privilege Card grants unlimited free chances.** |
| **Energy run** | Energy | Configurable per click: **5 energy = 1 run**, **10 energy = 2 runs**, **25 energy = 5 runs**. The yellow "×N" badge on the button shows your selected multiplier. |

## Hunt drop rates (from in-game Rules popup)

### Guaranteed every claim
| Reward | Rate |
|---|---|
| Gold | 100% |
| EXP | 100% |

### Gear pieces — 0.79% each

Three weapons and five slots × three sets (Destruction / Echo / Decisiveness).

> **Set role:** These are the **base/entry sets** that map to the three upgrade tiers in the main Gear system:
> - **Destruction → Dragoon** (base version)
> - **Decisiveness → Griffin** (base version)
> - **Echo → Oracle** (base version)
>
> They share similar bonuses to their upgrade-tier counterparts but more limited. Past early game, these pieces are mainly **used as upgrade fodder** to level Dragoon/Griffin/Oracle. See [gear.md](gear.md) for the full gear progression.

| Slot | Items |
|---|---|
| Weapon | Heroic Longbow, Beam Staff, Agile Knuckles |
| Armor | Armor of Destruction, Echo Armor, Armor of Decisiveness |
| Boots | Boots of Destruction, Echo Boots, Boots of Decisiveness |
| Helmet | Helmet of Destruction, Echo Helmet, Helmet of Decisiveness |
| Ring | Ring of Destruction, Echo Ring, Ring of Decisiveness |
| Amulet | Amulet of Destruction, Echo Amulet, Amulet of Decisiveness |

**Total: 18 gear pieces, 0.79% each.**

### Gear scrolls — 11.90% each

One scroll per slot type:

- Weapon Scroll
- Armor Scroll
- Helmet Scroll
- Ring Scroll
- Amulet Scroll
- Boots Scroll

### Runes — 0.60% each

Sawblade Circle, Spin SPD Up, Dragonflight Sword, Ricochet Strike, Sprite Multishot, Sprite Link, Sharp Arrow, Giant Meteor, Potion Magnet, Flamenox Seal, Frostshock Seal, Rootguard, Vine Bind, Circle, Ring of Agony, Sword of Time, Strike Potion, Melee Sprite, Healing Sprite, Arrow Rain, Star of Time, Star of Fury, Plant Summon, Equinox Bloom.

> These are **runes**, not in-run skills. Hunt deposits them straight into your rune inventory.

## Recommendation framing (for the bot)

- **Always claim Hunt before it caps at 24h** — wasted time = wasted rewards.
- **Quick Hunt advice depends on player tier:**
  - F2P: use the 2 free daily ad chances; don't burn energy on Quick Hunt.
  - Hunting Warrior Card owners: spam unlimited free Quick Hunts; energy-paid runs are situational.
  - Energy-paid Quick Hunts are most efficient at 25 energy / 5 runs (volume).
- Push deeper into the campaign to raise Hunt's hourly rate.
