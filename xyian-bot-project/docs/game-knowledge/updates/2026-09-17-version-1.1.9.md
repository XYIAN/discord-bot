## Update Log — Version 1.1.9

### New Content

1. **Mid-Autumn Special Event Series: Starmoon Vow** (Lasts 2 weeks)
   - **Angler's Bounty Special Edition**: Features special fishing zone **Fullmoon Pool**, with a higher chance of landing King Fisher Tickets! The event also brings the all-new Epic Mystling **Dragon Gate Koi**! (Event period: 09/19-10/02)
   - **Starfall Disc**: Win festival-limited Legendary skins! (Artemis - **Moonwake Shimmer**, and Demeter's new skin - **Silkveil Harvest**)
   - Plus 7-Day Sign-in and Alliance Treasure rewards!
2. The new Guild Expedition season opens October 5! It adds a new tier, **Starbound Eternity**, with rewards to match. Rally your guild and make a run at the highest honor!
3. New All-Star Cup & Mega All-Star Cup season theme: **Plant build** (starts 10/05, 0:00 UTC).
4. **Dragoon Set and Meteor build rune balance changes**: New affixes, new combat feel!
   Once changes go live, the Dragoon Set can be switched between its old and new versions - you can pick whichever suits your loadout and playstyle. The adjusted version is used by default after release and can be switched manually.
5. Etched Twinborn Runes now available.
6. New Campaign chapters: **201-210**, with difficulty reduced on Campaign stages past Chapter 100.
7. New Hard Mode chapters: **186-195**.
8. New Shackled Jungle stages: **1701-2150**.

### Rumble Ladder Improvements

1. Added mode shortcut on Home screen.
2. At the start of a match, the three players with the highest Charm are shown in an intro animation.
3. The results screen now shows the MVP and match statistics.
4. In Football Rumble, scoring 3 or more own goals in a single match now costs you Cred Score. Once your Cred Score is low, further penalties are applied on top.
5. In Football Rumble, quick emotes can be sent at kickoff and after a goal.
6. Mode availability changed to 1:00:00 - 4:59:59 and 10:00:00 - 17:59:59 (UTC).

### Arena Improvements

1. **Tier System Rework**
   - Arena will be restructured into 8 tiers: Bronze, Gold, Diamond, Master, Transcendant I, Transcendant II, Supreme I, and Supreme II.
   When the rework first goes live:
   - Players in the old Transcendant tier will move to [Transcendant I].
   - Players in the old Supreme tier will move to [Supreme I].
   *No one will be placed directly into [Transcendant II] or [Supreme II] based on their points before the rework.
2. **Matchmaking range improvements**: Evens out uneven competitive pressure across matchmaking ranges for a better overall experience. (Effective September 21, 0:00 UTC)
3. **Points rule improvements**: Improves cross-server arena matchmaking and points calculation. Challenge points are set by the actual points gap between both sides, and settlement uses the win and loss points fixed in the opponent list.
4. **New repeat opponent filter**: After you beat the same opponent twice in a day, that player is no longer recommended when you refresh the list.
5. **Defense loss protection**: Adds daily protection against point loss on defense. Once your accumulated loss meets the protection threshold, further failed defenses cost no points.
6. **Challenge list refresh changes**: Improves cross-server arena refresh experience. The first two refreshes are free, and the refresh cost resets after you complete a challenge.

_These changes apply to the standard Arena only and do not affect Peak Arena. Anything not noted otherwise takes effect as soon as the version releases._

### Guild Raid Improvements

1. Added **difficulty recommendations** based on the guild's total Battle Power.
2. Added **first-time auto-open**: If no one starts a raid within 24 hours of the current event cycle, the next member to open the Guild Raid screen starts one at the difficulty recommended for the guild's current total Battle Power.
3. Added **settlement countdown and reminder once conditions are met**, making it easier for officers to pace the run.

### Gameplay Optimizations

1. Added cosmetic previews - you can see how avatars, frames, titles, chat bubbles, personal cards, camps and battle scenes look before using them.
2. Added **"Again"** button to the pause screen in Campaign, All-Star Cup and Mega All-Star Cup official matches, letting you retry by spending Energy or a Ticket.
3. Added conversion function for some collab event items. Interdimensional Cores, Tidal Starcore-SPEC and Razor Starcore-SPEC can be converted into their matching items, and conversions can be done in bulk.
4. Friend list can now be sorted by chapter progress, Battle Power or last active, ascending or descending, making friends easier to find.
5. Improved daily deals purchase flow: When short on Aurocite, confirming takes you straight to the matching top-up purchase.
6. Reduced strength of Seraph boss.
7. Players can discard skills for free 3 times per day
8. Guild Expedition matchmaking now shows guild rankings. Top 50 guilds can display their rank
9. In Guild Expedition, viewing a member's preset now shows related progression info

### Bug Fixes

1. Fixed an issue where some buttons on the Home screen and tab animations reset incorrectly after switching screens.
2. Fixed an issue where Phynx's Bleed elemental resistance did not take effect.
3. Fixed an issue in All-Star Cup and Mega All-Star Cup where the Nian Beast boss's flame trail was not cleared when it passed through a room.
4. Fixed the revival screen counting down faster than it should.
5. Fixed an issue where Mystling Active Skill boosts were not applied to Sprite damage.
6. Softened the visual effect on the Wheat Field skill.
7. Fixed an issue in the [Umbral Tempest] season where holding Sword Strike Enchantment, Sword Strike Laser and Sword Strike Hit buff affixes at the same time applied the debuff to the player instead.
