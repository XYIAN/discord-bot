The list scrolls. # Statements from Kyle (guild owner), 2026-09-30

_Not on any screenshot — said in the session while sending the batches. Recorded in his words (typos tidied), to be filed with him as the source, not as screen captures._

1. **Tier colours confirmed.** Asked whether grey / green / blue / purple / yellow / red are Common / Fine / Rare / Epic / Legendary / Mythic and whether the numbered markers are Legendary +1 / +2 / +3: "yes that's all correct".
2. **Etched runes are only ever RECEIVED at blue or higher — but the lower tiers exist.** First said: "Etched runes start at blue as lowest — it's the only rune that's like that. It's because they are much more rare and harder to get." Then corrected in his next message, which is the version to file: "I was a little wrong. While we only receive etched runes starting with blue, they do have those levels, so those stats should persist for the lower tiers. For receiving these runes, all shops and events always give it to us blue or purple, and the rare exception of Legendary from the money shop — by money I mean real currency, which for US dollars is $100 for that bundle that has the Legendary etched, which you can pick which ones you want, once a month."
3. **How to get Etched runes.** "The best way is saving gems and spending it on lures during the Angler's Bounty monthly event, because you can purchase blue and purple etched runes there from the fish (weight) you catch."
4. **Icon shape.** "Etched runes are the diamond shaped ones like Sprite's Awe."
5. **Nickname.** Arrow of Echoes is "commonly known as AoE". _(Editorial: "AoE" is also the everyday abbreviation for area-of-effect; the entry will need to say which is meant.)_
6. **Twinborn runes.** "Twinborn runes [are] a rare rune type that's unlocked after obtaining your first Mythic rune, in which you can combine two runes of the same type and shape. The one I'll show you is two octagon (some refer to [it] as 'right side' because of where it's equipped on screen — I'll show you pics later)."
7. **Enchants.** "You'll notice those on non-etched runes.
