## Update Log

### New Mode: Airship Trade

Set sail with Guild members, escort cargo, and fight for resources across the trade world!

1. **Guild Trade**: Work with Guild members, compete for cabin slots, and escort cargo to the Central Trading Post to earn Gear, Characters, Runes, Mystlings, and more!
2. **Personal Trade**: Send out your Personal Airship and refresh cargo quality freely. Higher quality means better transport rewards!
3. **Trade Raids**: Find other players' Guild or Personal Airships on the trade map and start PVP battles. Defeat the guards to loot their cargo!
4. **Escort & Wanted**: Set a defense lineup to protect your cargo! If your Personal Airship is raided, place a bounty on the opponent and strike back!
5. **Trade World**: Explore the new trade map, view Airship routes in real time, share raid targets with other players, and compete for trade resources!

_*Airship Trade opens on October 19 (UTC)._

### Gameplay Improvements

1. Equipped or preset Twinborn Runes can now be dismantled, making Rune adjustments easier. Locked Runes must still be unlocked first.
2. Improved chat history loading.
3. Improved the avatar frame for Guild Expedition S5.
4. Reduced the difficulty of Shackled Jungle Stage 1600.
5. Fixed an issue where Griffin Amulet reflect damage could trigger twice; Griffin Amulet counterattack damage is now capped at player ATK*10000 per hit.

_(Screen 03 shows nothing below Gameplay Improvements item 5, but the text panel runs off the bottom of the frame, so whether the log continues past the visible area was not captured.)_
