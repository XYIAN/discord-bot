# Reach Rewards

The screen behind the **"Reach [chapter-wave]"** button on the main screen (e.g., "Reach 123-50"). Three sub-tabs along the bottom organize one-time rewards for campaign progression.

## Sub-tabs

1. **Clear Reward** (yellow crown — default selected)
2. **Hard Mode Clear Reward** (gold crown)
3. **Star Challenge** (red chest)

---

## Tab 1: Clear Reward

Rewards granted **one-time per wave reached** in the campaign.

- Chapter carousel — scroll to view rewards by chapter and wave
- Each wave milestone gives a fixed reward bundle
- **Example (Chapter 123, Wave 50):** 200 Gems, 5 Energy, 13 Gear Scrolls

> **Gear Scrolls are per-slot** (not generic). Each scroll is one of: Weapon Scroll, Armor Scroll, Helmet Scroll, Ring Scroll, Amulet Scroll, Boots Scroll. These are **the same resource that drops from Hunt** — Hunt's "Yellow scroll" icon represents one of these 6 slot-specific scrolls. Used to upgrade that slot's gear level (1–200).

**For the bot:** when a player levels up the campaign, remind them to open Reach Rewards and claim the new milestones.

## Tab 3: Star Challenge

Per-stage challenge objectives that grant up to **3 stars per stage**.

### How stars work
- Each stage lists **3 objectives**.
- Meeting an objective on a clear earns 1 star (3 stars max per stage).
- Common objectives:
  - "Clear time doesn't exceed [time]" (e.g., 09:30)
  - "Less than X attacks received" (e.g., 5 or 10)
  - "Doesn't trigger revive"
  - "Defeat more than X monsters" (e.g., 800)
- Each attempt costs **5 energy** (standard stage cost).

### Star Rewards (cumulative milestone payouts)

Claim rewards as your **total stars across all stages** crosses milestones:

| Stars accumulated | Reward |
|---|---|
| Every 3 stars (e.g., 306, 309, 312, 318, 321...) | 500 gems |
| Major milestones (e.g., 315) | 5,000 gems (10× bonus) |

Pattern: rewards start small at low star counts and **build progressively** as you accumulate stars. Periodic large-bonus milestones exist (e.g., 5,000 gems at 315). Exact cadence pending — revisit once a fuller milestone screenshot range is captured.

### Example stages and objectives observed
- **Stage 100 — Altar Ruins:** clear time ≤ 09:30 (and others)
- **Stage 101 — Silentwood:** clear time ≤ 09:30, less than 5 attacks, no revive
- **Stage 102 — Celestia Sanctum:** defeat 800+ monsters, less than 10 attacks, no revive
- **Stage 103 — Mired Abyss:** clear time ≤ 09:30, less than 5 attacks, no revive

## Recommendation framing (for the bot)

- After campaign progression, **always check Clear Reward** for unclaimed wave milestones.
- **Star Challenge is a slow gem income channel** — encourage attempting easier-stage stars first; the 5,000-gem milestones are worth chasing.
- Clear Rewards are one-time, so prioritize them anytime the player is approaching a wave they haven't been past before.
