# Runes

Accessed via the **Gear** bottom-nav tab → **Runes** sub-tab. Runes provide stat bonuses, starting skills, and special passives. They equip to a fixed set of slots arranged around the active hero.

## Equip slots (fixed counts per category)

Total equippable runes: **13** (when fully unlocked).

| Category | Max slots |
|---|---|
| Ability Runes | 4 |
| Enhancement Runes | 4 |
| Blessing Runes | 2 |
| Etched Runes | 3 |

> **New player gating:** Etched Rune slots #2 and #3 are locked until the player reaches certain rune-level thresholds. Slot #1 is available from the start.

## Rune categories (4 types)

Each category has its own dedicated slots. Roles:

- **Ability Runes** — grant Starting Skills (active in-run abilities)
- **Enhancement Runes** — amplify a specific theme/category (more on this below); their own internal sub-system that's substantially more complex than the other categories
- **Blessing Runes** — passive run-modifiers (luck, dodge, conversion, revive, etc.)
- **Etched Runes** — high-power category amplifiers, locking in build identity

## Rune categorization (7 themes)

**All Ability, Enhancement, and Etched Runes fall into one of 7 themed categories.** Blessing Runes are the exception — they sit outside this categorization.

The 7 themes:

- Circle
- Main Weapon
- Sword Strike
- Meteor
- Elemental
- Plant
- Sprite

This categorization matters because:
1. **Promised Ruins** lets you target by category — and the 7 themes split into two selection groups *only for that purpose* (see Promised Ruins section below)
2. **Etched Runes amplify by category** — a Sprite Etched Rune only multiplies Sprite-themed Ability Runes
3. **Strategic builds** align all 4 Ability + 4 Enhancement + 3 Etched runes around 1–3 themes

> The Group A / Group B split exists **only as the Promised Ruins selection mechanic**. Outside that screen, the 7 themes are flat — no need to think in terms of A/B groupings.

## Rune rarity ladder

Runes use a **different ladder than gear** — caps at **Mythic** (no Chaotic, no Mythic +variants).

1. Common
2. Fine
3. Rare
4. Epic
5. Epic +1
6. Epic +2
7. Legendary
8. Legendary +1
9. Legendary +2
10. Legendary +3
11. **Mythic** (max)

Color-coding visible on rune icons: white → green → blue → purple → higher tiers continue through the +levels.

> **Mythic-tier runes are realistically reachable for F2P** — but it takes a long time of accumulating duplicates from Hunt drops, Rune Ruins, Promised Ruins, and free chests. Spending players hit Mythic faster on their preferred runes. Legendary +3 on key runes is a comfortable mid-term F2P goal; Mythic is a long-term aim.

## How rune stats unlock by rarity

Each rarity tier on a rune unlocks **one new stat line**. The detail screen shows all stat lines, each preceded by a **colored dot** that tells you which rarity tier unlocked it.

### Dot color → rarity tier mapping

The colored dots represent **only the 6 named tiers** (not the +variants). Each named tier unlocks one new stat line; the +variants between named tiers bump cumulative values without adding new dot rows.

| Dot color | Rarity tier |
|---|---|
| ⚪ Gray | Common |
| 🟢 Green | Fine |
| 🔵 Blue | Rare |
| 🟣 Purple | Epic |
| 🟡 Yellow | Legendary |
| 🔴 Red | Mythic |
| 🔒 Lock icon | Not yet unlocked (above current rarity) |

**The +variants** (Epic +1 / +2; Legendary +1 / +2 / +3) sit **between** the colored tiers. They do not add new dot rows — they amplify existing effect values and bump the cumulative primary stat (knife/heart icon).

> **Enchantment slot unlocks at Epic +2** — this falls between the purple (Epic) and yellow (Legendary) dots, so it's not visible as a dot row; the Enchantment Stat appears as a separate section at the bottom of the rune detail screen once it unlocks.

The **primary stat row at the top** (knife icon = ATK PWR; heart icon = Max HP) shows the cumulative primary stat, which scales upward as the rune progresses through every tier including Legendary+ and Mythic.

### Worked example: Sharp Arrow ladder

Sharp Arrow (Enhancement Rune, Main Weapon theme) at Legendary +3 shows:

| Tier | Dot | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Common | ⚪ | Main Weapon DMG +5 |
| Fine | 🟢 | Main Weapon DMG +10 |
| Rare | 🔵 | ATK PWR +50 |
| Epic | 🟣 | Main weapon DMG +10% |
| Legendary | 🟡 | Main weapon DMG +20% |
| Mythic | 🔴 | Main weapon DMG +30% |
| Epic +1 / Epic +2 (between Epic and Legendary) | (no new dot row) | Value bumps; **Enchantment slot opens at Epic +2** |
| Legendary +1 / +2 / +3 (between Legendary and Mythic) | (no new dot row) | Cumulative primary ATK PWR scales toward Mythic max |

Plus a separate **Enchantment Stat** chosen from the Archive (currently equipped: Main Weapon CRIT Rate +30%).

> **Same structural framework applies to every rune** — read the dot colors in screenshots to extract per-tier unlocks. The 6 dot-rows establish a rune's full effect kit; +variants polish numerical values.

# Per-rune upgrade ladders

Every rune follows the same 6-tier framework. Tables below show what each tier unlocks. **Lines marked `[verify]` were cut off in the available screenshots** and should be confirmed.

> Reading note: Ability/Enhancement Runes have a primary stat at the top (heart for HP-leaning, knife for ATK-leaning) that scales with rarity. Blessing Runes show two primary stats. Etched Runes have larger numbers throughout. The 6 dot-rows below are the unique per-tier unlocks; the cumulative primary stat scales independently up through Mythic.

> **Methodology for reading locked Etched rows:** Lock-icon color tells you the tier band — yellow lock = Legendary band (L / L+1 / L+2 / L+3 — 4 rows), red lock = Mythic (1 row). Within the yellow band, top-to-bottom order maps to L → L+1 → L+2 → L+3. The single red-locked row is always Mythic. Locked text remains readable; the lock just gates ownership, not visibility.

### What progression delivers

The number of unlock lines depends on the rune category:

- **Ability Runes:** 6 unlocks total (one per named tier: Common → Mythic). +variants (Epic +1/+2, Legendary +1/+2/+3) only bump the cumulative primary stat — they do NOT add new dot lines.
- **Enhancement Runes:** Same as Ability — 6 unlocks total. +variants are stat-value bumps only.
- **Blessing Runes:** **4 unlocks total** (Rare → Mythic). Common and Fine are absorbed into the cumulative ATK PWR + Max HP at the top of the detail screen.
- **Etched Runes:** **Up to 9 unlocks total** — the 6 named tiers PLUS additional buff lines at **Legendary +1, +2, +3**. This is unique to Etched Runes; the L+ variants here meaningfully grow the rune's kit (not just numbers).

### Other progression rules

- **Mythic** = top tier: final unlock, max stat values, no further upgrades available.
- An **Enchantment Stat slot unlocks at Epic +2** — per rune (each rune individually needs to reach Epic +2). Once unlocked, the player uses the **Enchant** button to pick which Enchantment Stat to equip. Until a choice is made, the slot displays the placeholder text **"Unlocks Rune Enchantment"** (this is what Vine Bind shows even at Legendary +2 — slot is unlocked, just hasn't been enchanted yet). Etched Runes do NOT have an Enchantment slot.

> **Etched Runes do NOT have an Enchantment slot** — they only have an Equip button. The Enchantment system is only for Ability, Enhancement, and Blessing Runes.

> **Per-rune upgrade ladders are valuable for build planning** — knowing what each tier unlocks tells you whether a rune is worth pushing to Mythic vs leaving at Legendary. ## The 4 sub-tabs (Runes screen → Rune Workshop button takes you here)

A nav bar at the bottom of the rune-management screen has 4 tabs:

2. **Rune Ruins** — dig for random runes

### 1. Rune Workshop — Fusion

> **"Choose your runes, I will give you more power"**

Merge owned runes to produce a single higher-rarity rune.

- **Warehouse** view shows all available (non-equipped) runes
- **Quick Fuse** auto-selects optimal fusion candidates
- **Fuse** processes the selected runes and outputs a higher-rarity result
- **Fusion successful** popup shows: result rune name, new rarity (e.g., Epic), stat preview (old → new), and any newly unlocked stat lines

Equipped runes are excluded from fusion (they show "Equipped" overlays).

### 2. Rune Ruins — Dig for random runes

> **"Unearth ruins and unlock chests! Grand prizes belong to bravest, luckiest explorers!"**

- **Dig x1** — costs 1 Ruin Shovel (or fallback to Gems)
- **Dig x5** — costs 1,000 Gems
- **Hold down to auto-dig** for batch digs

#### Reward Chests

| Chest | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Rusty Chest** | Random white or green runes |
| **Sturdy Chest** | Random green or blue runes |
| **Glorious Chest** | Random blue or purple runes |

#### Drop rate detail (Rusty Chest example)

- Excludes min guarantee, overall rate **28.80%** at the rune-tier (15 individual runes at 1.11% each, then more at 0.31% / 0.30% / 0.15% for rarer items)

Higher-tier chests follow the same structure with higher rarity floors.

### 3. Promised Ruins — Targeted dig (by category)

Same chest framework as Rune Ruins, but **category-targeted**. **You pick ONE category from EACH of the two groups** (not "or") — so every Promised Ruins dig is guaranteed to draw from both your Group A and Group B selections.

**Group A — pick one:** Circle, Main Weapon, Sword Strike
**Group B — pick one:** Meteor, Elemental, Plant, Sprite

So if you select **Circle** and **Sprite**, your Promised Ruins runs target both Circle-themed and Sprite-themed runes.

- **Dig x1** — 300 Gems (or 1 Relic Shovel)
- **Dig x5** — 1,500 Gems

Use Promised Ruins when you're building toward two specific themes; use Rune Ruins when you're fishing broadly.

### 4. Pack — Paid rune packs

Two pack tiers:

- **Daily Special Pack** (refreshes every 24h) — 6 packs ranging from 499 Aurocite up to $49.99 USD, all flagged "400% Value"
- **Weekly Special Pack** (refreshes weekly) — additional packs at higher price points

Pack contents typically include: gems, gold, ruin shovels, relic shovels, runes directly, and rune-related upgrade materials.

### Enchantment system (Ability / Enhancement / Blessing Runes only)

**Etched Runes do NOT have Enchantments.** The Enchantment system applies to Ability, Enhancement, and Blessing Runes.

#### Mechanics

- **Unlocked at Epic +2** on each rune (per-rune unlock, not account-wide).
- Each rune has an **Enchantment Archive** — a pool of possible Enchantment Stats organized into **categories** specific to the rune type.
- **Only ONE Enchantment can be equipped at a time** per rune.
- Use the **Enchant** button on a rune to open the **Enchantizer** screen and swap which Enchantment Stat is active.
- The "Enchantment Stats" line on the rune detail screen always shows the currently equipped Enchantment.
- Until you make a selection after Epic +2 unlock, the line displays placeholder text **"Unlocks Rune Enchantment"**.
- A progression bar (e.g. 21/100) at the bottom of the Enchantizer suggests a level/exp track that unlocks additional Archive entries.

#### Enchantment quality

Within the Archive, each Enchantment entry has its own rarity (Common → Mythic). When you "pull" or roll a new entry, its quality ranges from Common up to Mythic depending on the pull. The **Specialized** category (see Blessing breakdown below) is always Legendary or Mythic — never lower.

- 21st–30th purchase: **300 gems/attempt**
- 31st–40th purchase: **400 gems/attempt**
- (further escalation likely above)

**The cost ladder resets daily** — you're back to the base 1st-attempt cost each day. So the strategy for heavy gem-to-Enchantium converters is to spread purchases across days rather than burning all in one sitting.

### Standard Enchantizer

The default pull mechanic. Each attempt rolls **3 random Enchantments** from the rune's eligible affix pool, displayed in a slot-machine-style 3-row UI. You then choose one to equip (or skip and re-roll).

#### Costs and pull mechanics

- **Cost:** 1,000 Enchantium per attempt
- **3 random affixes shown** per pull (displayed top-to-bottom)
- **"Skip" toggle** — when on, auto-rolls past any Common/Fine/Rare results
- **Auto-enchant** (hold down the Enchant button) — repeats pulls automatically and **pauses on any Legendary or Mythic result** (Rule 2 from in-game)
- **Anti-dup rule:** "When enchanting runes of the same type, identical Mythic affixes will not be drawn" — Mythic dupes are filtered

#### Drop rates (Standard Enchantizer)

Across all eligible affixes for a given rune:

| Quality | Approximate cumulative rate | Per-affix rate (observed) |
|---|---|---|
| Common | ~49.5% | 24.75% per option (2 options visible) |
| Fine | ~37% | 9.31% per option (4 options visible) |
| Rare | ~varies | 2.05% per option |
| Epic | ~2.25% | (verify per-option) |
| **Legendary** | **0.50%** total | 0.08% per option |
| **Mythic** | **0.25%** total | 0.06% per option |

So roughly **1 in 400 pulls is Mythic** at standard rates — a slow grind without help.

A premium variant of the Enchantizer accessed via a separate icon (the pink-circle Advanced Enchantium icon at bottom-right of the standard Enchantizer screen). Same UI, dramatically better odds.

#### Costs and pull mechanics

- **Cost:** 1 Advanced Enchantium per attempt (currency is much rarer than standard Enchantium)
- **Pity counter:** "Get Mythic affix in N draws" — guarantees a Mythic affix within a rolling pity window (observed at 3 draws)
- Same 3-row slot machine, Skip toggle, auto-enchant rules

| Quality | Cumulative rate |

| Epic | ~70% (verify exact %) |
| **Legendary** | **18.2%** |
| **Mythic** | **11%** |
| Per-affix Legendary | 3.03% per option |
| Per-affix Mythic | 2.75% per option |

So roughly **1 in 9 Advanced pulls is Mythic** — vastly better than the standard 1-in-400.

#### Earning Advanced Enchantium

- **1 Advanced Enchantium per 100 standard Enchant pulls** — the 21/100 counter at the bottom of the standard Enchantizer is the progress bar toward this earned conversion. So every 100,000 Enchantium spent on standard pulls earns you 1 Advanced Enchantium.
- **Direct purchase** — Advanced Enchantium can also be bought (currency / store details TBD).

So even pure-Enchantium grinders can slowly accumulate Advanced Enchantium passively (1 per 100 standard pulls), but it's a slow trickle.

### Enchantment Archive (the "affix archive")

> **Rule 3 (in-game):** "The affix archive stores unlocked affix types of the highest quality. They can be freely switched in unlocked rune slots, and saved as different presets."

Key implications:

- **The Archive remembers the highest-quality version** of each affix you've ever pulled. If you pull a Legendary "Boss DMG +5%" and later a Mythic "Boss DMG +15%", only the Mythic stays in the archive for that affix.
- Once an affix is in the Archive, **you can freely switch among them** (the Equip action) — no re-rolling needed.
- **Presets** can be saved per rune slot — so you can have different Enchantment configurations for different content (e.g., one preset for boss fights, one for crowd clear).
- The "Obtained" label visible next to certain affixes in the Rules popup indicates that affix is already in your Archive.

### Mythic Specialized clarification

The "Specialized" 7th-category Enchantment (each rune type has one) is the highest-value Mythic-only outcome. The Mythic Specialized roll for each rune is unique to that rune (see rune-specific Specialized tables in earlier sections). Pulling a Mythic Specialized via the Standard Enchantizer is roughly **1 in 1,667 pulls** (0.06% per Mythic-tier option × 1 option per category for Specialized) — extremely slow without Advanced Enchantium.

**Strategic implication:** The **Advanced Enchantizer's 11% Mythic rate plus its pity counter** is what makes endgame Enchantments feasible. F2P players accumulate Advanced Enchantium slowly; spending players or those with high event participation can outpace the grind.

### Strategic note: Ability vs Enhancement

You equip **4 Ability + 4 Enhancement** runes simultaneously. Ability Runes give you the building blocks (starting skills, summons, procs); Enhancement Runes amplify and reshape how those skills behave.

**Coherent builds pair themes** — e.g.:
- **Sprite build:** 2× Sprite Ability (Melee Sprite, Healing Sprite) + 2× Sprite Enhancement (Sprite Multishot, Sprite Link) + Sprite Etched (Sprite Assist, Sprite's Awe)
- **Circle build:** 2× Circle Ability (Circle, Ring of Agony) + 2× Circle Enhancement (Sawblade Circle, Spin SPD Up) + Circle Etched (Pulsing Orb, Orbital Orb)
- **Plant build:** Plant Summon + Equinox Bloom + Rootguard + Vine Bind + Life Surge
- **Meteor build:** Star of Time + Star of Fury + Potion Magnet + Giant Meteor + Meteor Split / Potion Spring

The 7-theme categorization is the backbone of every rune build decision.

### Inferred role: Blessing Runes

**Blessing Runes are passive run-modifiers** — they don't trigger abilities like Ability Runes. They shift probabilities (skill quality, encounter type, dodge), enable safety nets (Revive), or convert stats (Resilience). With only 2 slots, choosing your two Blessings is one of the highest-leverage build decisions.

### Inferred role: Etched Runes

**Etched Runes are category amplifiers** that lock in a build identity. They don't grant Starting Skills themselves (mostly) — they multiply whatever your Ability Runes produce. With only 3 slots, your Etched Runes essentially declare which 3 ability categories your build leans on (e.g., Sprites + Circles + Meteors).

### Build pattern

Match Etched Runes to your equipped Ability Runes:
- Running Sprite-themed Ability Runes (Melee Sprite + Healing Sprite) → equip **Sprite Assist** + **Sprite's Awe**
- Running Circle/Ring Ability Runes → equip **Pulsing Orb** + **Orbital Orb**
- Running Meteor Ability Runes (Star of Time + Star of Fury) → equip **Meteor Split**
- Running multi-category builds → spread Etched Runes thinner
