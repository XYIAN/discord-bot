# Daily Rewards (Great Value)

Accessed from the **Great Value** icon on the main screen left panel. Offers a daily-resetting set of value packs centered around hero shards.

## Top-level

- **Pack reset:** daily (~24h timer visible at top)
- **"Buy All Special Packs" bulk option:** 499 Aurocite for "Value 1700%"
- **Selected hero (Swap Character):** the current hero whose shards/themed rewards drive the pack contents (default seen: **Phynx**)
- Banner: "Receive +10% bonus Aurocite when purchasing from the Habby Store"

## The 4 daily packs

| Pack | Price | Value claim | Daily limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bonus Pack** | Free | — | 1/day |
| **Value Pack** | 99 Aurocite | 1700% | 1/day |
| **Featured Pack** | 199 Aurocite | 1350% | 1/day |
| **Deluxe Pack** | 299 Aurocite | 1200% | 1/day |

### Value / Featured / Deluxe Pack contents

Each pack contains a mix of items including hero shards, gold, gems, and a **Draw Item Choice Chest** which lets the player choose from: 1× Chromatic Chest key, 1× Wish Coin, 2× Relic Shovel.

## Rules

1. Packs are limited to 1 purchase per day and reset daily.
2. You can swap heroes for different pack options (the selected hero changes which shards drop).
3. Buying all packs at once gives a greater discount. Once you've purchased any pack individually, the bulk option becomes unavailable.
4. After purchasing any paid pack, you can no longer swap hero packs (locks in the selected hero for the day).
