# Events

The bottom-nav **Events** tab. Three sub-categories at the top, each with multiple sub-features:

1. **Arena** (PvP modes — 4 sub-modes)
2. **Challenge** (PvE challenges — Magic Plant Defense, Seal Battle, Sky Tower)
3. **Dungeon** (PvE farming — Abyssal Tide, Gold Cave, Shackled Jungle)

Each sub-feature has its own season/cycle, rewards, and shop. The Privilege Cards (Sealing Master, Cave Explorer, Abyssium Conqueror) directly enable Quick-Raid functionality on most of these.

A 1v1 ranked PvP ladder with a 5-tier promotion system.

### Tier ladder (from in-game Rules)

6 tiers total, low → high:

| Tier | Players per group | Promotion / Demotion |
|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | 50 | Top 15 promoted; rest stay |
| **Gold** | 100 | Top 30 promoted; bottom 40 demoted; rest stay |
| **Diamond** | 200 | Top 20 promoted; bottom 100 demoted; rest stay |
| **Master** | No limit | Top 20% promoted; bottom 40% demoted; rest stay |
| **Transcendent** | No limit | Top 20% promoted; bottom 40% demoted; rest stay (player's current tier — "Transcendent Battle" header) |
| **Supreme** | No limit | **Top tier — no promotion possible.** Tiers of Top 40% remain unchanged; the rest are demoted |

### Insufficient player rules

If a group has fewer players than expected during a weekly settlement, result priority is:

**Same-ranked players > Promoted players > Demoted players**

Example from the in-game Rules: a group with 45 players where 20 are first pegged to remain at same rank, then the top 20 to be promoted, and finally the last 5 are demoted.

- **Challenge attempts** are limited (visible: "Challenge x1" — likely daily-restored)

## 1b. Peak Arena

A 3-character team deployment PvP mode with a "Power of Fusion" diversity bonus system. Why you have Peak1/Peak2/Peak3 loadout slots — these are designed for Peak Arena.

### Ticket rules (from in-game Rules)

- Each challenge costs **1 Peak Arena Ticket**
- Tickets refill periodically (regen rate observed: 1 ticket per 3h 06m)
- Tickets can be purchased with gems
- Purchases reset daily
- ⚠️ **When each season ends, all excess tickets above the cap are removed.** Don't stockpile tickets at season end.

### Duration

- Daily 0:00–23:50 (UTC+0) — short daily downtime at end of UTC day

### Deployment rules

- Each player **dispatches 3 characters** to attack or defend
- Duplicate characters allowed
- **Active skin skills of deployed characters DO take effect**, but **Resonance skills do NOT**
- Players can equip each deployed character with gear and runes
- Gear/runes can be **reused across characters** (no need to own multiple sets)

### Power of Fusion (the diversity bonus)

The signature Peak Arena mechanic. **Power of Fusion** grants all deployed characters Final ATK and Final HP bonuses equal to **[Stacks × 1%]**.

Stacks are gained from diversity:

- **Unique characters:** When more than 1 unique character is deployed, each additional unique character grants **+10 stacks**
- **Unique gear pieces:** When more than 6 unique gear pieces are equipped among all deployed characters, each additional unique gear piece grants **+8 stacks**
- **Unique Runes:** When more than 13 unique Runes are equipped across all deployed characters, each additional unique Rune grants **+6 stacks**
- **Godforged Stars:** Each full Godforged Star on a deployed character's gear grants **+3 stacks**. For duplicate gear, only the highest Godforge star counts.

> **Strategic implication:** Peak Arena rewards breadth in your collection. Running 3 different characters with different gear, runes, and high Godforge stars stacks Power of Fusion meaningfully. This is the opposite of Arena (1v1 single-loadout) — Peak Arena requires you to invest across multiple builds.

### Ranking

- New seasons start with no rank — must **win at least 1 battle** to be ranked.
- **Stair-step structure** displayed on the screen (ranks 48 / 50 / 53 / 61 visible)
- Player snapshot: **Rank 61 (Faukss XYIAN, 19.17M power)**
- **Daily rewards** claimed 2/2 visible — daily reward cap

### Buttons

- **Refresh** — re-roll opponents
- **Team Preset** — apply a saved loadout
- Bottom tabs: **Rankings** / **Shop** (Peak Arena Shop) / **Battle Report**

### Peak Arena Shop

Tab inside the Arena Shop area; uses **yellow star icon coins** (Peak Arena Coins). **Refreshes every ~2 days** (in sync with seasonal cycles).

## 1c. Rumble Ladder (Free-For-All Rumble)

A 6-player free-for-all PvP survival mode with two sub-modes (Rumble Dome / Free-for-All) and strict time windows.

### Gameplay rules (from in-game Rules)

- **6-player matches** — you and 5 other players enter the map
- **3-minute matches** — gain power by collecting EXP, defeat monsters to earn gifts, deliver gifts to storage zones to score points. Compete for top rank within 3 min.
- **Two sub-modes:**
  - **Rumble Dome** — ATK and HP **scale with player current power**
  - **Free-for-All** — default ATK and HP used; only appearance and Rumble-specific skills inherited (so no power gap from gear/runes — pure skill match)

### Map features

- **Storage Zone** — deliver carried gifts here to score points
- **Trap Zone** — contains traps; gift barrels refresh every 30 seconds
- **Boss Zone** — 1 boss spawns every 30 seconds; first boss after 30s

### Characters & skills

- Players use owned characters; **each character has unique Rumble skills**
- New skills acquired during battle don't interrupt the skill panel — panel stays open and skills can be selected at any time
- If no skills selected, new skills queue

### Trophies & matchmaking

- Trophies are calculated **separately for each character** — each character has its own trophy count
- Trophies increase or decrease based on each character's individual performance + match placement
- **Total trophies** for ranking are summed from your **top 3 characters' trophies**
- Matchmaking prioritizes players of similar rank using the multiplayer **Cred Score** system

### Timing window (from in-game Rules)

**Free-For-All Rumble runs Mon–Wed only**, in 4 specific 3-hour windows per day (all UTC+0):

- 1:00 – 4:00
- 7:00 – 10:00
- 13:00 – 16:00
- 19:00 – 22:00

So a typical 12 hours/day across 3 days = 36 hours total per week. Currently **Off-Season** in the snapshot — Rumble runs in cycles.

| Stage | Format | Advancement |

- **Solo or 2-player co-op (duos)** — difficulty rises in a 2-player team
- **The host uses a ticket** to start the mode; assisting players do NOT use tickets
- **Max 10 assist rewards per week** — cap on helping out
- **3 tickets replenished weekly** + **1 ticket can be earned from daily tasks** (so a solo grinder gets up to ~10 tickets/week as host)

### Stages

- Magic Plant Defense unlocks new stages **based on campaign progress**

## 2b. Seal Battle

A boss-based PvE challenge with seasonal structure and rotating bosses.

### Season structure (from in-game Rules)

- **Seasons last 28 days** total
- **Each boss lasts 7 days**, with **4 boss rounds** per season (4 × 7 = 28)
- **Settlement phase: final 10 minutes** of each boss round — challenges are not allowed during settlement
- **Current season:** Seal Battle Season 21
- The 09d 06h timer in the snapshot = **time remaining in the current season**, not the season length

### Gameplay rules

- **EXP drops randomly on the field** during runs — players must actively collect drops to upgrade during a run
- **2 challenges per day** (Sealing Master Privilege Card adds +1)
- Rewards are based on **survival time** during a run
- **Revival is not allowed** in this mode — exception: revival gear

### Grouping and Ranking

- Players are **grouped based on registration time** when participating
- Each boss round's group ranking is based on **total DMG to that boss**
- At the start of each new boss round, groups are **re-ranked** based on the previous round's standings
- At the end of a season, groups are **demoted** based on previous season's group rank — different demotion quotas per rank

### Tier ladder (Seal Battle has its own tier system, similar to Arena)

The system uses banded ranking tiers (icons across the top of the ranking screen):

- **Demon Slayer** (purple — **top tier** — currently the player's tier)
- **Elite** (yellow)
- Lower tiers (blue, etc. — TBD)

> **Important:** "Demon Slayer" is the **tier**, NOT the theme/name of the season. Each tier has its own ranking group, with rewards scaling by tier (top-tier ranks earn substantially more).

# Strategic guidance (for the bot)

- **Arena modes layer different rewards** — running all 4 PvP modes yields different shop currencies and shard pools (e.g., Arena Coins for Phynx/Nezha; All-Star Cup tokens for Universal Artifact Shards).
- **Peak1/Peak2/Peak3 loadouts** in your gear system map directly to Peak Arena — that's why they exist as named slots.
- **Privilege Cards multiply Challenge/Dungeon dailies** — Sealing Master, Cave Explorer, and Abyssium Conqueror are highest-leverage cards for active event grinders.
- **All-Star Cup themes change every season** (Sword Strike now → next theme TBD). Build presets aligned to the active theme for tournament viability.
