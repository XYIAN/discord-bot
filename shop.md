# Shop

The bottom-nav **Shop** tab. Contains everything purchasable in the game, organized into 5 top-level sub-tabs.

## Sub-tabs (left to right)

1. **Daily Shop** (default)
2. **Gear Chest**
3. **Wish**
4. **Limited Offer**
5. **Top Up**

---

## 1. Daily Shop

**Refreshes every 24h.** Items rotate daily — different gear/scrolls/shards each refresh.

### Featured top of screen
- **Chapter Pack** — a one-time-only USD pack tied to a specific campaign chapter (e.g., "Ch. 122 Gift Pack — 600% Value — $99.99"). Unlocks per chapter milestone.

### Daily Shop grid (rotating)
6 items per refresh. Each shows "Purchases left: 1" and a price.

Examples observed:
| Item | Discount | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Helix Shard | (sold out) | — |
| Amulet of Decisiveness | 30% off | 50 Gems |
| Helmet of Destruction | 30% off | 40 Gems |
| Helmet Scroll | — | 42 Gems |
| Weapon Scroll | — | 500 Gold |
| Helmet Scroll | — | 700 Gold |

> Items can be base-set gear (Destruction/Decisiveness/Echo), gear scrolls, or hero/character shards. Currency varies — gems and gold both appear.

## 2. Gear Chest

Four chest types. **Pulls cost keys** (1 key = 1 pull) OR can be purchased with **Gems** if you're out of keys. Each chest type uses its own key.

### Key types

| Key | Chest |
|---|---|
| Gray key | Silver Chest |
| Purple key | Obsidian Chest |
| Purple-faded-to-blue key | Mythstone Chest AND Chromatic Chest (shared) |

### Mythstone Chest
- **Contents:** Rare or Epic gear; chance of obtaining 5-star Epic gear increased
- **The "5★ Epic Gear" tier = S-Ranked gear at Epic minimum.** S-Ranked pieces (Dragoon/Griffin/Oracle) only drop at Epic rarity or higher; they never drop as Common/Fine/Rare. See `gear.md` for the full rarity ladder.
- **Pity (from in-game Rules):** see Rules popup — base pity values not explicitly captured. Pity counters in-game are **per-account**, so the "X opens to guarantee" numbers in your account are user-specific.
- **Series sub-tabs:** Griffin / Oracle / Dragoon — pick the series you want to pull from
- **Pull cost:** 1 purple-blue key per pull (or pay in Gems)

**Drop rates** (excludes min guarantee, totals 100%):
| Tier | Overall | Per item | # items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5★ Epic Gear | 1.46% | 0.24% | 6 |
| Epic Gear | 2.18% | 0.12% | 18 |
| Rare Gear | 38.18% | 2.12% | 18 |
| Fine Gear | 58.18% | 3.23% | 18 |

### Chromatic Chest
- **Contents:** Rare or Epic gear; chance of obtaining 5-star Epic gear increased
- Same S-Ranked-at-Epic-minimum rule as Mythstone — the "5★ Epic Gear" line in the drop table = S-Ranked gear (Dragoon/Griffin/Oracle).
- **Pity:** Pity values shown in-game (Epic within X opens; 5-Star Epic within Y opens) are user-specific counters, not base rates. Re-check the Rules popup for any explicit base values.
- **Pull cost:** 1 purple-blue key per pull (shared with Mythstone) or pay in Gems

**Drop rates:**
| Tier | Overall | Per item | # items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5★ Epic Gear | 1.44% | 0.08% | 18 |
| Epic Gear | 2.18% | 0.12% | 18 |
| Rare Gear | 38.18% | 2.12% | 18 |
| Fine Gear | 58.18% | 3.23% | 18 |

### Silver Chest
- **Contents:** Common or Rare gear
- **Free draw cadence:** 1 free pull per 24h
- **Pull cost:** 1 gray key (or pay in Gems)

**Drop rates:**
| Tier | Overall | Per item | # items |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fine Gear | 33.33% | 1.85% | 18 |
| Common Gear | 66.67% | 3.70% | 18 |

### Obsidian Chest
- **Contents:** Rare, Epic, or Legendary gear
- **Free draw cadence:** 1 free pull every ~2 days (showed 1d 06h)
- **Pull cost:** 1 purple key (or pay in Gems)
- **Pity:** Obsidian does have a pity counter (base value not captured); the values shown in-game on a player's screen are their personal progress toward that pity, not the base threshold.

**Drop rates:**
| Tier | Overall | Per item | # items |
|---|---|---|---|
| Epic Gear | 4.00% | 0.22% | 18 |
| Rare Gear | 40.00% | 2.22% | 18 |
| Fine Gear | 56.00% | 3.11% | 18 |

> When the guaranteed prize is triggered, all item drop rates within that tier are equal.

## 3. Wish

Pull artifacts from a "wish" mechanic with a small **angel-like character** animation.

### Mechanics
- **Pull cost:** "Wish coins" (currency from packs/events)
- **Pull options:** Wish x10 or Wish x100 (e.g., 16/10 = you have 16 wish coins, x10 pull costs 10)
- **Pity (user-specific counters):** the in-game display showed Epic Artifact guaranteed within 18 chances; Legendary or Mythic within 78 chances. **These are the player's current pity counters, not base values.** Confirm base rates from the Rules popup.
- When guarantee is triggered: 90% Legendary / 10% Mythic. Within a rarity, all artifacts have equal appearance rates.

**Drop rates:**
| Tier | Overall | Per item | # items |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mythic Artifact | 0.10% | 0.02% | 5 |
| Legendary Artifact | 1.00% | 0.11% | 9 |
| Epic Artifact | 9.00% | 1.29% | 7 |
| Rare Artifact | 18.00% | 3.60% | 5 |
| Epic Artifact Shard+ | 24.00% | 3.43% | 7 |
| Rare Artifact Shard+ | 47.90% | 9.58% | 5 |

### Daily Special Pack section (within Wish tab)
- 3 packs visible, refresh daily
- Mix of Aurocite-priced and USD-priced (e.g., 99 Aurocite, $9.99) at "400% Value"

## 4. Limited Offer

Four sub-tabs of paid bundles at varying price tiers. Each pack typically shows a "% Value" badge advertising the discount vs. buying contents separately.

### 4a. Daily Special — refreshes every 24h
6 packs, mix of Aurocite (e.g., 99) and USD ($9.99 / $19.99 / $25.99 / $49.99). Most flagged "400% Value".

### 4b. Weekly Special — refreshes once per week
9+ packs at 450% Value, ranging Aurocite 499 → USD $99.99. (The 03d 06h timer I observed was time remaining on the current week.)

### 4c. Monthly Special
9+ packs at 500% Value, mostly $9.99 → $99.99.

### 4d. Supreme Collection (Supreme Treasure Store)
- Refreshes daily (06:59 timer)
- All 600% Value
- All $99.99 packs (high-spend tier)

> **For F2P players:** ignore Limited Offer entirely. **For paying players:** Daily Special is highest value-per-dollar; Supreme Collection is whale tier.

## 5. Top Up

Direct currency purchases. Three sub-tabs: **Gems / Gold / Aurocite**.

### 5a. Gems (paid with Aurocite or USD — see currency note below)

| Pack | Gems | Cost | First-purchase bonus gems |
|---|---|---|---|
| Some Gems | 80 | 99 Aurocite | +80 |
| Pile of Gems | 500 | 499 Aurocite | **none** |
| Heap of Gems | 1,200 | $9.99 | +1,200 |
| Bag of Gems | 2,500 | $19.99 | **none** |
| Barrel of Gems | 6,500 | $49.99 | +6,500 |
| Chest of Gems | 14,000 | $99.99 | +14,000 |

> **Currency display behavior:** the game treats Aurocite as the default real-money currency (think dollars-to-crypto). If you have enough Aurocite to cover the price, it'll show in Aurocite. If not, it falls back to USD (or local currency). So the same pack can appear priced differently depending on your Aurocite balance.

## Recommendation framing (for the bot)

### F2P players
- **Daily Shop:** Always check, especially for discounted gear/scrolls under 100 Gems.
- **Silver Chest:** Claim free daily pull every 24h.
- **Obsidian Chest:** Claim free pull every ~2 days.
- **Wish:** No specific timing advice — you can use the x10 or x100 pull functions whenever you prefer.
- **Skip:** Limited Offer, Top Up, Chapter Pack, paid Mythstone/Chromatic pulls.

### Paying / open-to-paying
- **Best dollar-per-value:** Daily Special packs and Supreme Treasure Store at $99.99 (600% Value).
- **Aurocite buying:** Always use Habby Store for the +10% bonus when possible.
- **Top Up gold packs:** Skip — gold is easy to farm; conversion rate is bad.
- **Mythstone/Chromatic Aurocite pulls:** Pull on the series matching your active gear progression (Griffin / Oracle / Dragoon).
