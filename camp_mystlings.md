# Camp & Mystlings

Accessed via the **Camp** button on the bottom-left of the main screen action bar (next to the Start button). Contains a customizable town with several buildings and the Mystling collection system.

## Camp overview

- **Camp is named by the player** (e.g., "Fauksville"). Naming is unlocked at 2 Mystling collection progress.
- 3 main buildings visible on the Camp screen:
  - **Pact Sanctum** (currently Lv.120 for the player)
  - **Magic Plant Defense**
  - **Hatchery**
- Camp manual + Mystling buttons in the bottom-right corner

## The 3 Camp buildings

### Pact Sanctum

The building that determines the **passive ATK and HP buffs gained when any Mystling is deployed**.

- **"Pact effects gained when Mystling is deployed"**
- **Current values (Lv.120):** ATK Increase +8,020, HP Up +32,080
- **Next-level preview:** ATK +8,780, HP +35,420
- **Upgrade duration:** ~42 minutes per level at Lv.120 (scales with level)
- **Upgrade cost:** Gold + **Spirit Pact Seed** (blue plum-seed-shaped item)
- **Effect:** the gains apply globally to your active hero when ANY Mystling is in your deployed set

### Hatchery

Where you hatch new Mystlings.

- **8 visible egg slots** arranged in a circle (slot count may scale with collection progress — Mystling collection unlocks "+2 hatchery slots" at 5 progress)
- **Bulk Hatching** button — process multiple eggs at once
- **Hatching duration** is reduced by Mystling collection milestones (e.g., −720s at 10 collection progress)

#### Bulk Hatching options (from in-game popup)

When using Bulk Hatching, you have two configurable options:

**Choose Mystling Egg priority:**
- Higher rarity first (default for grinding stronger Mystlings)
- Lower rarity first

**Choose Aspect (Mystling category):**
- **Demon** / **Undead** / **Nature** / **Monster**

> "Guaranteed to hatch Mystling of chosen Aspect" — pick the category to fill, and Bulk Hatching only produces Mystlings of that category. Useful for filling out specific aspect collections.

### Magic Plant Defense

A combat sub-mode set within Camp, framed around protecting a glowing seedling/egg.

- Currently at **Stage 191** for the player
- Two challenge modes: **Solo Challenge** and **Team Challenge**
- **Drop Chance** preview shows 4 reward type icons (gem/material types)
- **Ranking** sidebar shows top players (player visible: Faukss, BigTKUncle, Mxfalls, etc.)
- Has its own **Exchange Shop** and **Tasks** sub-features (icons on right side)
- **Recruit chat** for team challenges visible at the bottom

## Mystlings system

Mystlings are collectible creatures that grant **Active Skills** in battle and **passive Collection Stats**.

### 4 Mystling categories

The Mystling inventory is organized by elemental type:

- **Demon**
- **Undead**
- **Nature**
- **Monster**

### Per-Mystling structure

Each Mystling has a **fixed rarity** (Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary / etc. — like heroes, locked to one tier per Mystling).

Each Mystling shows on its detail screen:

- **Name + star rating** (e.g., Death Mage, ★★★★★) — stars represent **upgrade level**, not rarity. Each Mystling has its own fixed rarity tier; stars are the ascension level you've reached on that Mystling.
- **Active Skill (Lv. 1+)** — a deployable battle ability. Example: Death Mage Lv. 1 fires 8 giant scythes, each dealing 160% character ATK and applying one of {ATK −10%, ATK SPD −10%, MOV SPD −10%, Dodge −10%} for 5s; reapplying refreshes the duration.
- **Collection Stats (Lv. 1+)** — a passive bonus from owning the Mystling. Example: Death Mage Lv. 1 Final DMG Bonus +2%.
- **Star Up** button — invest shards/duplicates to raise stars (per-Mystling — ascends that one's level)
- **Deploy** button — select for the active battle slot
- **Heart icon (per-Mystling page)** — shows total stars that Mystling has been upgraded across all instances/copies you own

> **Two different heart icons in the Mystling system:**
> - On the **Mystling detail page**, the heart shows total stars a specific Mystling has been upgraded
> - On the **Collect page**, the heart shows your overall **collection progress** (the milestone track number, e.g., 492/2600+)

### Deployment

Mystlings dispatch to support your hero. Number of Mystlings you can deploy at once is capped:

- **Base deploy cap: 1** Mystling
- **Sacred Hall privileges** add **+1 Max deployed Mystlings in Peak Arena, Arena, Guild Expedition** at higher Sacred Hall tiers (multiple instances)
- **Mystling collection milestones** add **+3 Max Mystling dispatches** at 20 progress (and likely more at later milestones)
- The base of 1 scales up over time through both progression tracks combined

## Mystling Collection Progress (account-wide buffs)

The **Collect** button on the Mystling screen opens a milestone reward track. Total Mystling collection progress (counted via heart/star icons on the Mystling cards) unlocks **permanent account-wide bonuses** at thresholds.

> **Player snapshot:** 492 collection progress

### Pattern observations

- **ATK PWR +15% and Max HP +15%** repeat at multiple thresholds (stacks meaningfully across collection)
- **Pact Sanctum upgrade time −5%** also repeats
- **Hatchery / hatching / dispatch cap upgrades** front-load in early collection progress
- **Combat-defensive bonuses** (DMG REDUC, prevent player DMG, active-skill DMG bumps) layer in throughout

> **Strategic implication:** The Mystling collection track is a **massive permanent power source**. It's account-wide, doesn't reset, and complements both Sacred Hall and rune progression. Hatching new Mystlings — even if you never deploy them — pushes your collection counter and unlocks more global buffs.
